How the clock moves
Every tap of “1 day” advances both calendars by one day. The Tzolk’in cycles through 13 numbers and 20 day signs (260 combinations). The Haab’ has 18 periods of 20 days plus 5 Wayeb’ days (365 total). Together they form the Calendar Round.
